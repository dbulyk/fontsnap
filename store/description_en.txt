FontSnap is an AI font identifier from image that works right inside Chrome. See a nice typeface on a website, in a banner or in a screenshot? Drag a box over the text or right-click the picture, and this font identifier tool shows the five closest Google typefaces.

🚀 How to identify font from image in 3 steps
1️⃣ Click the FontSnap icon in the toolbar or press Alt+Shift+F.
2️⃣ Drag a rectangle over a line or two of text, or right-click an image and choose Identify font in this image.
3️⃣ Read the top 5 matches with live previews and match scores.

No account and no server upload. The neural network runs locally, so this font identifier from image keeps your pictures on your computer.

❓ FAQ
📍 What is FontSnap?
It is a Chrome extension and AI font identifier that recognizes typefaces in a selected area of the screen, in an image on a web page or in an uploaded picture.

📍 How do I identify font from image files?
Open FontSnap on any page, switch to upload mode and paste, drop or choose a picture with text.

📍 Can I check a picture on a website without saving it?
Yes. Right-click the image and choose Identify font in this image.

📍 Does FontSnap work offline?
Recognition itself runs locally. An internet connection is only used to load previews from Google Fonts.

📍 Can FontSnap recognize commercial typefaces?
FontSnap matches against Google Fonts families. For a commercial typeface it shows the closest Google Fonts alternatives, which is often what you need for a web project.

📍 Which languages are supported?
Latin, Cyrillic and Arabic scripts give the most accurate results. Chinese and Japanese support is still limited.

📍 Is my data collected?
No. The extension does not collect personal data or send your images anywhere.
